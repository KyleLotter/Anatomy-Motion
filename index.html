    Credits and sources

    3D models

    Organ models are from the Human Reference Atlas 3D Reference Object Library (HuBMAP, male and female sets v1.2, mammary glands from v1.3), built from the Visible Human male and female datasets of the US National Library of Medicine. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.

    Modified from the originals: meshes were merged, simplified to between 2% and 100% of their triangles and compressed for the web, then drawn as points and lines.

    Stylised stand-ins, not from scans

    The library has no stomach, oesophagus, skull, ribs, arm bones or peripheral nerves. Those are simple hand-built shapes, drawn dashed and tagged “stylised”. Their size, position and paths are approximate.

    The male and female bodies are scans of two different people, so organs differ in size and position between them. Stand-ins are stretched to fit each body.

    Not included at all: arm and leg blood vessels, lymph vessels, muscles, and hand and foot bones. The female set has no urethra; the male set has no testes.

    Facts

    OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e; NIDDK (kidneys, digestive system); American Heart Association (resting heart rate); Azevedo et al. 2009 (neuron count); Raichle & Gusnard 2002 (brain energy use).

    Pulse, breathing, filtration and brain-wave readouts are simulations inside normal resting ranges. They are not measurements.
